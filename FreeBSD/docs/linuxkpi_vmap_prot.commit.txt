linuxkpi: Honor the pgprot argument of vmap()

vmap() ignored prot, so pgprot_writecombine() callers got a cached
mapping.  On Meteor Lake the i915 media GT does not snoop the CPU
caches and its GuC rejects the firmware read through such a mapping.

Apply a non-default cache mode with pmap_change_attr(), and restore
each page's own attribute in vunmap().

Tested on Meteor Lake (i915 media GT, VA-API) and Strix Halo (amdgpu).
