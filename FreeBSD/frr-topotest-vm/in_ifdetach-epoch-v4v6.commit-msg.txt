netinet, netinet6: wait for the net epoch before freeing the lltable

in_ifdetach() and in6_ifdeparture() freed the lltable while the ifnet was
still reachable by index, so an RTF_LLDATA RTM_DELETE (or netlink
RTM_DELNEIGH) racing with interface destruction used freed memory.
Clear the pointer, NET_EPOCH_WAIT(), then free, as before 0d469d23715d.
