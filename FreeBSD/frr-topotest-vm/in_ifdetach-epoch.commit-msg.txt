netinet: wait for the net epoch before freeing if_inet

in_ifdetach() freed if_inet and its lltable while the ifnet was still
reachable by index, so an RTF_LLDATA RTM_DELETE racing with interface
destruction used freed memory.  Clear if_inet, NET_EPOCH_WAIT(), then
free, as before 0d469d23715d.
