# Review of the NFS jail patches, 2026-11-24

Seven reviewer subagents, one per commit, at xhigh effort, followed
`~/myscripts/.agents/skills/freebsd-src/review.md` (the CI review prompt
plus its seven local checks). Each was given only its commit and goal, not
the author's own concerns. Every finding below was then checked against
the code before being fixed.

Verdict legend: **confirmed** (code path read or reproduced), **consistent**
(matches an earlier measurement), **plausible** (reasoning holds, not traced
end to end).

## Findings and what was done
